WHO I AM

01

I'm a technology enthusiast and researcher based in Kathmandu, Nepal, with over 8 years of experience in banking, and AI research. Having an academic background in Engineering, My work sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and financial systems — a space I find endlessly compelling.
